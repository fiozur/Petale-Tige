Care & Botanical Longevity

Cut stem bases diagonally at a 45-degree angle with clean floral shears under cool running water. Replace the vase water every 48 hours to prevent bacterial buildup.
Place your bouquet in soft, indirect daylight. Avoid placing near direct heat registers, drafty air conditioners, or ripening fruit which emit ethylene gas.
Dissolve half of the provided organic floral nutrient packet on day one, and the remaining half when replenishing clean water on day three.
