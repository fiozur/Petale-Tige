Our Origins & Ethos

Rooted in Slow Floristry & Gentle Design.

Founded in 2021 by floral designer Éléonore Vance, Pétale & Tige was born as an antidote to industrial flower commerce. We believe flowers should never feel mass-produced, stiffly packaged, or hurried.

Instead, our studio operates as a tactile workshop. Every dawn, our florists personally choose each stem from regenerative heritage nurseries in the Île-de-France and Kent valleys, valuing natural bends, asymmetric grace, and wild perfume over sterile uniformity.

Pillars of Our Craft

The Three Guiding Principles

100% Floral Foam Free

We refuse microplastic floral foam. All installations and vase bouquets rely on Japanese kenzans, chicken wire, and recyclable natural twigs.

Compostable Unboxing

Wrapped in recycled unbleached kraft paper, tied with unrefined mulberry silk remnants, and accompanied by plant-based hydration pillows.

Heritage Seed Cultivation

We partner directly with small independent growers who revive heirloom garden varieties with natural fragrances that commercial hybrids lost decades ago.
